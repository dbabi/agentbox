# Security policy

agentbox is early alpha. Do not use it to protect anything that matters yet.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report security problems privately. Do not put any details in a public issue.

1. Open the repository's **Security** tab.
2. Choose **Report a vulnerability**, or go straight to `https://github.com/dbabi/agentbox/security/advisories/new`.

What agentbox protects, the trust boundaries and the known limits are in [docs/wiki/Security-Model.md](docs/wiki/Security-Model.md) and [docs/THREAT-MODEL.md](docs/THREAT-MODEL.md). A report that finds a way past one of the stated limits is still welcome.
